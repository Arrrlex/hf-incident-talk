The article credits Amodei and Clark’s [Faulty reward functions in the wild](https://openai.com/index/faulty-reward-functions/) (OpenAI, 21 December 2016).

Downloaded on 9 September 2026 from the [article’s GIF asset](https://storage.googleapis.com/gdm-deepmind-com-prod-public/media/original_images/622755a90ba8e091464f819f_Fig204.gif). # Interpretability circuit

`interpretability-circuit.png` reproduces Anthropic’s simplified Dallas–Texas–Austin circuit from [On the Biology of a Large Language Model](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attribution-graphs/biology.html#dives-tracing) (27 March 2025). The model studied is Claude 3.5 Haiku. This is the paper’s simplified explanatory figure, not a complete computational graph or a result from Astra.

Downloaded unchanged on 9 September 2026 from the [original image](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attribution-graphs/png/img_4e42fad1d7624d1e.png). Embedded locally for offline presentation and export.
