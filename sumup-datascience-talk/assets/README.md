# Lie-detector comparison

`lie-detector-transfer.png` reproduces panel (e) of Figure 1 from Cooney, Africa and Irving, [“Did you lie?” Evaluating Lie Detectors across Model Scale and Belief-Verified Model Organisms](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.12618v2) (June 2026), UK AI Security Institute. The paper is licensed CC BY 4.0. The panel has been cropped from the page, with its data, labels and colours unchanged.

Rendered on 9 September 2026 from page 2 of the [v2 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.12618v2), using `pdftoppm -f 2 -l 2 -scale-to 3600 -x 1714 -y 1066 -W 580 -H 616 -singlefile -png`. Embedded locally for offline presentation and export. “Trained MO” means trained model organisms. The strong CoT-monitor result partly reflects selection of models whose deceptive beliefs were visible in CoT; this qualification appears on the slide and in the notes.
